Langpao Island

ONE BREATH

Freedive the reef on one breath, take a fish with your speargun, and sell it to Joe before dark.

The first launch compiles the shaders and can take a minute or two. Later visits load much faster.
Tip

Press G for the speargun and hold R to pull a band back. Shoot under water only: a gun's range is about three times its length.

Glide in slowly. Fish bolt from a diver finning hard, and let one that hangs still come closer.

Aim just behind the eye for a stone shot. A big fish runs: don't fight it on one breath. Press G to let go of the gun, and with a float line pull it in from the surface.

A grouper or snapper may wedge itself into the reef. Pulling only tears the shaft out: go up, breathe, and come back to reach in.

Parrotfish, Nassau grouper, tarpon, turtles and rays are protected, and yellowtail must be 30 cm. The park ranger fines you for the rest.

The danger of a long dive is the way up: the oxygen in your lungs thins as the pressure falls. Breathe up at the surface between dives, and turn back at the first contractions.

Joe at the fish stand by the pier buys your catch. Fish are worth more by weight, so bring the big ones in.

Marta at the boathouse sells gear: longer guns, better shafts, a reel, fins, a freediving course, a bigger hold and a fish finder.

Press E at the boat to board, at the wheel to take the helm, and again to step away and walk the deck.

Press I for your cooler and the fish log. Deck lights from Marta light the water for night dives.
